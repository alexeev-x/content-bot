Privacy Policy — ALEXEEV Studio

Effective date: 15 October 2026. Last updated: 15 October 2026.

1. Who operates the service

ALEXEEV Studio is a personal project operated by Alexeev, a private individual and the author of the @alexeev_x channels on Telegram, TikTok and YouTube. For any question about this policy or your data, write on Telegram: @alexeev_x.

2. What the service is

ALEXEEV Studio is a private dashboard the operator uses to prepare and schedule posts for the operator's Telegram channel and to see how those posts and videos perform. Only the operator can sign in; it is not open to the public and has no user registration.

Working today:

  • writing, storing and scheduling posts with media, and sending them to the operator's Telegram channels;
  • statistics of the operator's own public Telegram channel and YouTube channel (followers, views, likes, comments);
  • statistics of the operator's own TikTok account, once the operator connects it (section 4). The connection runs in TikTok's developer sandbox and is not an approved public integration.

3. Data the service processes, by source

  • Sign-in (Telegram). The operator signs in with the Telegram Login Widget or the Telegram Mini App. The service checks the Telegram account ID against the single allowed operator ID and stores sessions: a hash of the session token, the Telegram ID, the session type and its times.
  • Content. Posts, their text, attached images and videos, publication schedules, the Telegram channels they are sent to, delivery results and an activity history of changes in the dashboard.
  • Telegram channel statistics. Public numbers from the channel's public web page (t.me/s/alexeev_x): subscriber count, and the text, date, preview image, views and reaction counts of recent posts.
  • YouTube statistics. Public data of the operator's YouTube channel read with the YouTube Data API: subscriber count, and the title, date, thumbnail, views, likes and comment counts of recent videos. No YouTube account sign-in is used.
  • Technical data. To limit abusive requests, the service counts requests per connecting IP address for short time windows. The hosting provider records request logs; the service's own logs are structured events (operation, status, error code, duration) and do not contain tokens, cookies, sign-in payloads or message bodies.

4. TikTok

The operator can link the operator's own TikTok account through TikTok's official sign-in (Login Kit). Linking is optional and happens only when the account holder approves it on TikTok. The service requests these permissions:

  • user.info.basic and user.info.profile: account ID, display name, avatar and profile details;
  • user.info.stats: follower, following, likes and video counts;
  • video.list: the account's public videos with their titles, dates, cover images and view, like, comment and share counts.

The data is used only to show the operator their own statistics in the dashboard; it is collected about once an hour. Access and refresh tokens issued by TikTok are stored on the server only, encrypted, and are never sent to a browser. Numbers from TikTok can be delayed or incomplete. Authorization can be revoked at any time in the TikTok app settings, in the list of apps that have access to the account. ALEXEEV Studio is not affiliated with, sponsored or endorsed by TikTok.

5. Why the data is processed

  • to let the operator sign in and keep other people out;
  • to store, schedule and publish the operator's posts;
  • to show the operator how the posts and videos perform;
  • to keep the service secure and to diagnose failures.

The data is not sold, not used for advertising profiles and not shared for marketing.

6. Service providers

  • Cloudflare hosts the service: its Workers platform runs it, its D1 database stores the records and its R2 storage keeps uploaded media. Cloudflare processes request data to deliver and protect the service.
  • Telegram provides sign-in and receives the posts the operator publishes.
  • Google (YouTube Data API) provides the public YouTube statistics.
  • TikTok provides account data once the operator connects an account.

7. How long data is kept

  • Sign-in sessions: the browser refresh session lasts up to 30 days; ended sessions are deleted about a day later.
  • Request counters for abuse protection: about one hour.
  • Published posts moved to the archive: deleted 30 days after archiving.
  • Delivery attempt details: 90 days. Resolved incidents and the activity history: 365 days.
  • Channel and video statistics, unpublished posts and channel settings: kept while the operator uses the service; they are deleted when the operator deletes them or on request.
  • Hosting provider logs: kept according to Cloudflare's own retention.

8. Security

Access is limited to one Telegram account. API credentials are kept as server-side secrets and are never included in web pages or the app bundle. Session tokens are stored only as hashes. Connections use HTTPS.

9. Cookies and browser storage

The service sets one cookie: an HTTP-only refresh cookie that keeps the operator signed in. The browser's local storage keeps interface preferences: the colour theme, whether the side menu is collapsed, the last channel chosen for publishing and the signed-in name. The access token is kept in memory only. The service uses no advertising or analytics trackers.

10. Your choices and requests

Anyone whose data appears in the service (for example, in a public channel statistic) can ask what is held, ask for a correction or ask for deletion by writing on Telegram to @alexeev_x. The «Отключить» (Disconnect) button on the analytics page removes the stored TikTok tokens and asks TikTok to revoke them; other requests are handled manually. TikTok authorization can also be revoked directly in TikTok as described in section 4.

11. Age

The service is a tool for the operator and is not directed at children.

12. Applicable law and changes

The operator processes personal data in line with the data protection law of the Republic of Moldova. This policy will be updated when the service changes, for example when the TikTok integration is approved for public use; the effective date at the top will change with each update.
