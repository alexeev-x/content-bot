# 7. Working quotas and cost

The planned load is up to 1,000 **background jobs/day** (a target, not measured); in the first month realistically 20–50. Limits we design for (**Workers Paid since 29.10.2026**, $5/month; owner: nothing above it, a $0.50 overage budget alert mails the owner):

- Workers Paid: 10 M requests and 30 M CPU ms a month included; **30 s CPU per invocation** by default (network wait is not counted); 10,000 subrequests and 1,000 D1 queries per invocation; 128 MB memory. Cron once a minute = 1,440 invocations/day. Most existing jobs were sized for Free (10 ms, 50 subrequests) and stay safe.
- The per-tick batch is bounded by subrequests and D1 queries: per job ≈ claim + mark + send + outcome write. With a batch of a few jobs, the capacity of thousands of jobs/day is above the target — **but this is a calculation, not a measurement**; it is confirmed by a load test.
- D1 on Workers Paid: 10 GB per database, 5 GB stored and **50 M rows written and 25 B read a month** included, Time Travel 7 days (Free was 500 MB and 100,000 written / 5 M read a day for the whole account, every query failing past it until 00:00 UTC). The hero statistics base has its own database (`docs/backend/sources.md`): ≈ 0.2 M rows written a day at its caps. A write counts each row it changes plus a row per index the write touches. Production measured 28.10.2026 (`wrangler d1 insights`): 40,500 rows written a day, 77% of them the analytics collection rewriting every post each hour; since then it rewrites only changed posts, every hour only the newest twenty and those younger than a week, the whole list once a day; the watch jobs (treasure, Steam, game files, hero matches) keep no `ai_queue_jobs` rows (about 3 300 a day), and the Cron heartbeat is written every five minutes (minutes ending in 2 or 7) or at once on a failure (about 1 150 fewer). Expected production without the match base: about 10 000–14 000 rows a day. DEV's Cron writes nothing while «Фоновые задачи» is off. The «Гайд на героя» match base adds about 26 rows per match (`docs/backend/sources.md`). Workers Logs Free: 200,000 events/day, retention 3 days (from 01.12.2026 — a new model: 0.5 GB/day, 7 days).
- Queue (if needed): 10,000 operations/day on Free, ≈ 3 operations per message, retention 24 h.

The number of subscribers does not equal the number of Bot API requests: a post to a channel or group is sent once. Telegram limits sending to a group (about 20 messages per minute) — a 429 response with `retry_after` is handled as `rate_limited`. Large media go to R2, not D1. Image generation on the Free plan (10 ms CPU) lives outside the Worker; the decision comes after measurements.
