## Compact and convenient: concrete comparisons

- Density: Linear list rows are one line (title, status, assignee, date) with display options for grouping/ordering, not bigger cards (https://linear.app/docs/display-options). Our Posts table already matches at 1280; Publications phone cards do not (P7).
- Primary action: Primer allows one primary per view and pairs danger with confirmation (https://primer.style/components/button). We break it in Settings (O1) and the draft editor (P1).
- Status: GitHub shows one banner and a vertical component list on the same page (https://www.githubstatus.com/). We hide the list behind a bare line (O2).
- Scheduling: Buffer's composer offers timing choices in one place and confirms with «Schedule Post» (https://support.buffer.com/article/642-scheduling-posts). We have no schedule action in the editor (P2).
- Phone: Telegram Mini Apps provide BackButton, MainButton and safe-area CSS variables (https://core.telegram.org/bots/webapps); we draw our own (P8).

Not verified: Typefully and Attio pages were not fetched; their patterns are not cited. Vercel dashboard status could not be confirmed from search.
