# §3.11 Рубрики

## 3.11 Рубрики `/rubrics`
- **Purpose:** when each of the channel's rubrics goes out and why, in Moscow time (the channel's slots are Moscow slots). Stage 3 shows the forecast only; the scheduler's own controls (switch, pause, rest, «Пропустить слот») come with its later stages (docs/scheduler/MASTER-PLAN.md §13 answer 16: the section is built to the maximum).
- **Banner:** «Это прогноз» while the scheduler only forecasts: the plan is computed on each opening, counts the posts already in «Публикации», and event rubrics appear when their event happens.
- **Calendar:** «Месяц» | «Список» (SegmentedControl, local state). Month: Monday-first seven columns, each forecast day a cell with its slots («19:04 Признавайтесь» plus picture and poll marks with text for screen readers); days outside the 35 are dashed. Under 720 px the days stack and only the filled ones show. List: the filled days, each slot with its reason («Отдых 14 дн. прошёл»). No «Неделя» view (owner, 31.10.2026).
- **Unavailable rubrics:** a Disclosure «Не могут выходить на этом сервере: N» naming each rubric and what it lacks (Browser Rendering, ИИ, STRATZ, база матчей).
- **States:** loading — a calendar-shaped skeleton with `aria-busy`; error — concise message, request id, retry (a safe GET); empty — «В ближайшие 35 дней рубрик нет» with the reason; 401/403 as everywhere (shell).
- **Switch (stage 4):** «Автопубликация рубрик» on top: its state (Работает / Выключена / Остановлена / Только прогноз), the switch «Рубрики выходят сами», the channel (production; locked until one is chosen), the next rubric, when the plan was last computed, and each lock in plain words with what to do.
- **Tabs:** «Календарь» — «Ближайшие слоты» (the real slots of the next week, each a 44 px row opening a side panel with the reason, the fallbacks, every build and release step and «Пропустить слот» behind a confirmation), then the forecast; «Рубрики» — every rubric with its state (Выходит / Банк исчерпан / Не хватает сервиса / Ждёт своего этапа / Выключена), last and next post, an on/off switch and its rest in days (7–60) with «Сохранить».
- **Navigation:** «Рубрики» in the sidebar and in «Ещё» on a phone.

- **Tab «Аналитика» (stages 7–8):** «Между рубриками» — a table per period (7/30/90 days): rubric (and the part it stood out by), posts, «к обычному» (median score, ×1,0 = the channel's usual post), the previous period, views, reactions, comments, votes, and «лучшая»/«худшая» (only when two or more rubrics have ≥ 3 posts) or «мало данных»; footer: the usual post and how many posts are scored, maturing, without data; a row opens the rubric below; compact list on a phone. «Часы» — weekday × Moscow hour (30/90 days), the number always in the cell (tone by steps, never colour alone), under three posts only their count. «Обучение» — the mode (advice / learns by itself / off), from when it applies, the switch «Менять отдых и часы самому после 4 недель», each pool rubric's rest with advice, the slot hours by day group. «Внутри рубрики» — rubric and period pickers, its usual post against the channel, slices (hero, bank line, look, picture/text, hour, weekday) better/worse inside the rubric, posts with their Telegram link and «посчитан / дозревает / без данных». «Что пишут в комментариях» — per rubric: count, kinds, up to three topics, one quote, and honest states (the bot cannot see comments, no themes yet, AI did not run, some comments left out). States: skeleton, error with request id and retry, 503 «нужна миграция 0121/0122», empty. Every rare state is shown on DEV at `/lab/rubrics`.
- **«История» per rubric (31.10.2026, owner):** a button on each rubric row opens a drawer with its last 30 slots: when (MSK), state pill and why it did not go out in Russian (`rubricSlotReason`), or the plan's reason when it did; a slot opens its steps (the slot drawer). States: skeleton, error with request id and retry, «Слотов ещё не было.».
- **«Лента дня» (Обзор):** a rubric post is a rounded-square marker with «Р» and «Рубрика «…»» in its tooltip and label; a rubric slot with no publication yet (being prepared, or not out) is a dashed one with its state and reason, and opens «Рубрики».
- **`/lab/rehearsal` «Репетиция» (DEV only):** the week-long test's journal — totals (как задумано / ошибки / не проверяются / ждут итога), «Показать только ошибки», each slot with its staged failure, the note, the slot state and the verdict; a row opens the slot drawer. Not running: one panel saying how it is switched on.
